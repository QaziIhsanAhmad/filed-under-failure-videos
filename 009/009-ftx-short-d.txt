FTX repaid customers 105%. But their bitcoin was priced at $16,871. #Shorts

FTX customers have been repaid 105% of their claims, but claims were valued in dollars on the 11 November 2022 petition date, when bitcoin was worth $16,871. Judge John Dorsey wrote that claims must be valued as of the petition date as if the bankruptcy had never occurred.
