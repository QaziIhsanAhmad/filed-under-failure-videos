FTX customers got 105% of their claims back. Its founder still got 25 years. #Shorts

FTX customers have been repaid 105% of their claims (FTX Recovery Trust, after the July 2026 distribution), yet Sam Bankman-Fried is serving 25 years. The Second Circuit said customers were defrauded as soon as their money went to Alameda, and upheld the trial judge's ruling that intent to repay was immaterial as a matter of law.
