FTX collapse explained from the court, SEC and bankruptcy records.

FTX customers have now been repaid 105% of their claims, yet Sam Bankman-Fried is serving 25 years. Using the Second Circuit's June 2026 opinion, the US Attorney's releases, the SEC's complaint, John J. Ray III's sworn bankruptcy declaration, the Delaware bankruptcy court's rulings, the FTX Recovery Trust's distribution notices and the Supreme Court docket, we explain the code privileges, the nine-day run, the trial, the repayments, why claims were fixed at November 2022 prices, and why the courts said repayment does not undo fraud. SEC claims are allegations; the criminal findings are the jury's and the courts'. The Supreme Court has not decided whether to hear his petition.
