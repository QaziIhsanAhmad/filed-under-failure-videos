Fosun agreed to invest £450m in Thomas Cook. After the collapse it paid £11m for the brand #Shorts

In August 2019 Fosun agreed to put £450m of new money into Thomas Cook. After the 23 September collapse, it bought the Thomas Cook brand for £11m.
