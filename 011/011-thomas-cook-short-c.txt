Most stranded Thomas Cook customers had no ATOL cover. Taxpayers paid an estimated £83m #Shorts

The National Audit Office found 55% of the Thomas Cook repatriation cost, £83m, fell on the Department for Transport because those customers had no ATOL protection. The department had first assumed 40%.
