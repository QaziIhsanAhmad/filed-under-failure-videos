Sunny Balwani and the Theranos collapse explained from the court record: why the other Theranos conviction stuck.

Elizabeth Holmes was acquitted of defrauding patients; her former number two, Ramesh 'Sunny' Balwani, was convicted on all twelve counts. An appeals court even agreed a witness at his trial gave 'actually false' testimony, and he still lost; on 5 October 2026 the Supreme Court declined to hear his case. Using the Ninth Circuit's December 2025 amended opinion, the Supreme Court docket, the US Attorney's sentencing release, the federal lab regulator's March 2016 sanctions letter and trial reporting, we follow the lab he oversaw, the patients, the pitch to investors, the two trials and the appeal. SEC claims are allegations; criminal findings are the jury's and the courts'. 'Our analysis' is labelled as our own reading.
