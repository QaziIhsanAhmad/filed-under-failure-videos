The regulator's Theranos letter named 'Ramesh Balwani, Owner'. Here's what it said. #Shorts

The federal lab regulator's letter of 18 March 2016 proposing sanctions against Theranos's Newark lab was addressed to the lab director, Elizabeth Holmes and Ramesh Balwani as owners. It cited immediate jeopardy to patient health and proposed a ban of at least two years on owners and operators.
