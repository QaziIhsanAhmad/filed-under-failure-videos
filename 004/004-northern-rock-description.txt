Northern Rock collapse explained from court and regulator records: the 2007 bank run that opened Britain's 2008 financial crisis.
Savers withdrew £4.45bn in three days. Using the Treasury Committee's 'The run on the Rock', the Bank of England's statements, the FSA's own internal audit and enforcement notices, the National Audit Office's reports and the shareholder rulings up to Strasbourg, we show why a bank that was solvent on paper ran out of cash, which executives the regulator found failed to correct, or repeated, understated bad-loan figures, why shareholders were paid nothing, and what the rescue finally cost.
