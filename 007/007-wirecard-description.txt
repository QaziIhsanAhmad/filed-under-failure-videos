Wirecard collapse explained from the audit, regulator and court records.

In June 2020 Germany's payments star admitted that €1.9 billion, about a quarter of its balance sheet, probably did not exist. Using Wirecard's own announcements, KPMG's special investigation, BaFin's orders, the Bundestag inquiry, the auditor watchdog's sanctions and reporting from the Munich trial, we explain how the 'third-party acquiring' business worked, why the regulator filed a complaint naming journalists, who has been held to account and where the trial stands. Markus Braun denies all charges and is presumed innocent; no verdict has been reached.
