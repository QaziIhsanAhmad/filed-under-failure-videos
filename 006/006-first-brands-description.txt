First Brands collapse explained from court and bankruptcy records.

The owner of Fram, Autolite and Raybestos sold about $5 billion of car parts a year. In September 2025 it filed for bankruptcy with $12 million in cash and more than $9 billion in liabilities. Using the US Attorney's charges, bankruptcy filings and lenders' own statements, we explain how invoice finance works, what prosecutors allege happened, what is still disputed, and who paid. Patrick and Edward James deny the charges and are presumed innocent; their trial is scheduled for February 2027.
