Silicon Valley Bank collapse explained from regulator records.
On 9 March 2023, customers withdrew more than $40 billion from Silicon Valley Bank in a single day; the next day it was closed. Using the Federal Reserve's 2023 review, its inspector general, the FDIC and a new 2026 independent review, we trace what went wrong, who saw it coming, and who paid.
