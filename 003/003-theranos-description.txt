Theranos collapse explained from court and regulator records.
A jury convicted Elizabeth Holmes of defrauding investors, and acquitted her of defrauding patients. Using the Ninth Circuit's February 2025 opinion, the SEC's 2018 charges, the federal lab regulator's findings and reporting on the verdict and sentence, we show what the court record actually established: 12 tests on the Edison out of 300 developed, demos running a 'null protocol', drug-company logos added to reports, and a projected $230m profit against $1.94m of reported income.

Context: A24's documentary 'You Can See Everything' (Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim) about Holmes opens in US cinemas in October 2026. This episode is independent of the film and uses no footage, stills or quotes from it; it sticks to the court and regulator record.
